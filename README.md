# ICAS-CSVD related brain structural damage

## Background
Cerebral small vessel disease (CSVD) related covert damage can coexist with intracranial artery stenosis (ICAS). 

## Purpose
We aim to explore whether the spatial distributions of CSVD markers are diverse in people with or without ICAS

## Core Datasets
- MRI (T1/T2/FLAIR sequences)
- Vascular imaging (MRA)
- Laboratory profiles
